Foesong — Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Foesong does not collect any data about you. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and the game does not send anything anywhere. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What the game stores

Foesong saves your duelists and your audio settings to the app's own private storage on your device, so the game is where you left it next time you open it. That information never leaves the device. Nobody, including the developer, can see it.

Deleting the app deletes it along with everything else the app holds.

What the game sends

Nothing. Foesong is a single-player game with no network features. Once it is installed it does not need a connection and does not make one.

Children

Because Foesong collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version of the game ever does collect something, this page will say so before that version is released, and the date above will change.
