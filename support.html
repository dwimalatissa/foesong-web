Foesong — Support

Last updated 9 September 2026

Foesong is made by one person. Mail dul.wimalatissa@gmail.com with anything at all — a bug, a duel that felt wrong, a build that will not start — and you will get a reply from the person who wrote it.

What helps when reporting a problem

  • Your device and iOS version.
  • The version shown in the corner of the title screen.
  • What you were doing — which arena, which opponent, what you pressed.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Saved games

Your duelists live in the app's own storage on the device, and nowhere else. There is no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes them and they cannot be recovered.

Playing in a browser

There is a browser version at dwimalatissa.github.io/foesong-web. It is the same game and it saves separately from the app.

Privacy

Foesong collects nothing. The full policy is on the privacy page.
